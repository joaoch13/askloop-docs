The short version

Askloop runs on Atlassian Forge and is eligible for the "Runs on Atlassian" programme. It has no servers of its own. Everything it stores stays inside Atlassian's platform, for your Confluence site, and it sends no data to us or to anyone else. About people, it stores only Atlassian account ids, never names or email addresses.

What Askloop stores, and where

Askloop keeps its data in the Forge SQL database that Atlassian hosts for the app on your site. Only Askloop can read it; it is not shared with other sites or other apps.

  • Questions: title, details, the space and (if asked on a page) the page id, topic, the account id of the person who asked, vote and answer counts, which answer is accepted, the "verified until" date and whether the question is published on a FAQ page.
  • Answers: the text, the account id of the person who answered and, once verified, of the person who verified it, and the dates.
  • Votes: "I have this question too" and "Helpful", as the voter's account id.
  • Topics: name, description, optional space key, the account ids of the experts, the optional group names for who may ask, who may answer and moderators, the verification period and the publishing and notification switches.
  • History: for every change to a question or answer, when it happened, the account id of the person who made it and what kind of change it was. When a question or answer is deleted, its text is deleted too; the history then records only that it was deleted.
  • FAQ pages and settings: the id of each FAQ page the app wrote, and the app's own bookkeeping (where the daily and weekly jobs stopped).
  • Account list: the account ids stored above, kept so they can be reported to Atlassian (see Personal data).

Question and answer text is stored as you typed it. Please do not put personal data there that you do not need.

What Askloop writes to Confluence

Askloop also writes to Confluence itself, through Confluence's own APIs. What it writes there is normal Confluence content, governed by Confluence's permissions and retention, not by the app.

  • FAQ pages. When an expert or moderator publishes an accepted answer (or a topic publishes automatically), Askloop writes it to a page called "FAQ: <topic>" in the space, with the question, the answer, and a mention of the person who answered and of the person who verified it. The page can be read by everyone who can view that space, and Confluence search and Rovo find it. Answers asked on a page with view restrictions (on the page or a parent page) are never published there. Every time the FAQ page is rewritten (and the daily check rewrites each FAQ page in turn), Askloop checks each question's page again and takes an answer off when its page was restricted, moved to another space or trashed. Answers that changed, or that the person who asked accepted in place of an earlier one, are shown labelled "Not verified" until an expert checks them.
  • Page history. Confluence keeps every earlier version of a page. When an answer is taken off a FAQ page (withdrawn, deleted, or its source page restricted), its text remains readable in the FAQ page's history by people who can view that page. Askloop cannot delete page versions: that needs a Confluence delete permission the app does not ask for. A space administrator can delete old versions from the page history.
  • Page comments, only if switched on. Askloop sends no email. If a topic has "Notify by page comment" on, Askloop adds a comment to the question's page that @mentions the experts (for a new question, or an answer due for review) or the person who asked (for a new answer), and Confluence notifies them as it does for any mention. The comment contains the question title and the mentions. Off by default, and only for questions asked on a page.

Who sees what

Confluence decides. Askloop checks, as the person using it, on every request: a question asked on a page is shown only to people who can view that page (page restrictions included), and a question asked in a space only to people who can view the space. Topics can further limit who may ask and who may answer to a Confluence group. Confluence administrators and a topic's moderators group can edit and delete any question or answer in it.

Personal data

Askloop stores the Atlassian account ids of people who ask, answer, vote, verify, are experts or appear in the history. It does not store names or email addresses: names are shown by Confluence's own user component each time.

Once a week Askloop reports the account ids it stores to Atlassian's personal data reporting API, as Atlassian requires. When Atlassian answers that an account has been closed, Askloop removes that account id everywhere in its own data: questions and answers stay and are shown as written by "a former user", and that person's votes and expert roles are deleted. Mentions on FAQ pages and page comments are Confluence content and follow Confluence's own handling of closed accounts.

Your organisation remains the controller of the data Askloop stores; we never receive it. Atlassian processes it under its own terms and privacy policy.

When the app is uninstalled, Atlassian removes the app's stored data under its Forge data retention rules. Export the questions to CSV first (Askloop settings) if you want to keep them. FAQ pages and page comments stay in Confluence like any other page.

What we receive

Nothing from your Confluence content or from the questions and answers. Askloop makes no calls outside Atlassian. Like every Marketplace partner, we can see the licensing and install information Atlassian provides to partners (for example which sites have the app installed and the licence tier) and the logs Forge keeps for the app. Those logs record counts and errors, and an error may contain a question, page or space id. We use this information only to support and improve Askloop.

Askloop holds no security or privacy certification of its own. It relies on Atlassian's platform, where its data is hosted.
